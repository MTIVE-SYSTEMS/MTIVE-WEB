MTIVE Industries · PANOPTES

Awareness beyond the soldier's own senses.

PANOPTES is a helmet- and glasses-mounted perception system for the dismounted soldier. Its 4 sensor channels scan without pause and resolve into a single quiet heads-up display.

Request briefing See what each sensor sees ↘

Product · How PANOPTES is worn

Sensors on the head, compute on the back.

The helmet and glasses carry the sensors and the display. A compact unit on the back does all the processing. A bracelet on the forearm takes the commands.

Head · Helmet and glasses

4 sensor channels scan the scene from the helmet and glasses.

  • CH 01Cameras, eye level
  • CH 02Motion
  • CH 03Thermal, always on
  • CH 04UWB radar, front-facing
→
Back · Processing unit

Onboard AI turns raw returns into a few clear marks.

ARGUS, MTIVE's perception AI, detects people, fuses the 4 channels and decides which returns are worth interrupting the soldier's view.

All processing on the onboard unit · No cloud · No reach-back link

→
Glasses · Heads-up display

The glasses show only what is worth the soldier's attention.

Solid boxes for confirmed detections, dashed boxes for unconfirmed signals. Nothing else reaches the glass.

→
Forearm · EMG bracelet

A forearm bracelet turns muscle signals into commands.

Gestures go to the processing unit. No voice, no buttons, hands stay on the weapon.

Company · MTIVE Industries

Built for the person on the ground.

MTIVE Industries is a Turkish defense technology company. It builds AI-powered perception systems that give the dismounted soldier awareness past the reach of eyes and ears.

ARGUS is made completely from scratch and optimised for this mission.

Every model is trained on data MTIVE owns, with no borrowed pretrained weights. Allied and sovereign customers get a stack with no foreign licensing strings and no export-control exposure.

Everything runs on the soldier's own equipment.

All processing happens in the compact unit worn on the back. PANOPTES needs no cloud and no reach-back link to work.

PANOPTES informs the operator and never decides for them.

Human-in-the-loop by design. It sharpens the operator's judgement and leaves every decision with the person wearing it.
