01 Flagship platform — Panoptes

Four channels. One picture.

Warfare with unfair advantage: Panoptes is MTIVE's flagship sensing platform for dismounted personnel, named for Argus Panoptes, the all-eyed giant. Sensing beyond what eyes and ears pick up alone, fused into a single display output. No channel below is built into hardware yet. Each is scoped honestly against what it can and can not do today.

CH.1

Camera

Feeds Vigil, the shared detector. The only channel with real, measured performance behind it — covered in full in the technical report.

Software — shipped
CH.2

Motion

Frame-differencing and optical flow. Catches subtle movement — disturbed grass, a shifted branch — too slow or small for a human eye to register.

Software — concept
CH.3

IR / thermal

Scans continuously, whether or not it is shown on the display. No thermal hardware is currently owned; a fusion concept was tested on synthetic data only.

Concept — not built
CH.4

UWB radar

Planned, not built. Directional, front-mounted, aimed with the cameras — for through-wall presence and breathing detection at a range affordable radar can actually reach.

Concept — not built

Output concept: a solid box marks a confirmed detection. A dashed or differently colored box marks an unconfirmed signal — motion-only or radar-only. An alert layer decides what actually interrupts view; sensors scan constantly, the display stays quiet until something earns attention.

02 Second platform — SmartPatrol360

Autonomy that keeps working when the network doesn't.

SmartPatrol360 is MTIVE's GPS-denied autonomous ISR platform: visual-odometry and SLAM navigation, edge-native sensor fusion, and a one-operator-to-many-drone model, built to keep operating when satellite positioning, comms, or a centralized network are degraded or denied.

01

GNSS-denied navigation

Visual odometry and SLAM keep the platform operating in GNSS-denied zones, enclosed facilities, and dense urban environments where GPS-dependent drones lose positional stability.

02

Edge-native compute

An NVIDIA Jetson AGX Orin handles inference and sensor fusion on-device, cutting the latency a cloud round-trip would add between detection and response.

03

LiDAR + thermal fusion

3D LiDAR perception combined with low-light and thermal sensing pipelines for day and night operation.

04

Secure comms

COFDM-based links with FHSS-enabled resilience and hardware-based entropy generation for encryption.

05

1:N operator model

One operator supervising a coordinated fleet, instead of the conventional near-1:1 pilot-to-drone ratio.

06

Digital-twin validation

Built and stress-tested in NVIDIA Omniverse and Isaac Sim before physical deployment.

Honest status

SmartPatrol360 is at TRL 4: validated in Isaac Sim simulation, not yet demonstrated in physical field deployment. MTIVE's technical track is supported by acceptance into the NVIDIA Inception Program — a technical-enablement relationship, not a commercial partnership.

04 Honest tracking, not oversold claims

A box does not vanish. It is timed.

"Box vanished on screen means a person is hiding" was rejected as a claim before any code was written — unfalsifiable on video, and the weak leg of the concept. What is built instead: a tracker that holds identity through a real occlusion, in three honestly labeled states.

Confirmed

Confirmed

Solid green. Seen this frame — a live, current detection.

Predicted

Dashed amber. Holding identity through a gap it is not currently seeing.

Lost — 00s

Dashed red with an elapsed counter. Not seen since the tracker's patience ran out — last known position, stated plainly.

A deliberately weaker, more defensible claim than most comparable pitches make. Made on purpose.
Verified, not assumed

An early Panoptes-vs-YOLO comparison auto-selected whichever track had the most hits per model independently — silently comparing two different moments in the same clip. Caught, root-caused, and fixed by anchoring both backends to one manually-verified event. Corrected finding: Panoptes held the subject across 74 confirmed detections in that window; YOLOv11n produced a single low-confidence flicker during a motion-blurred pan. The methodology fix mattered more than the number.

06 What a detector alone can not do

Every frame is the first frame it has ever seen.

A detector has no concept of time. "A box that was not there yesterday" is not a hard problem for a plain detector — it is impossible for one. Three architectural gaps sit between the current detector and the platform's actual goal.

00

Detector — Vigil

Built and measured this week. Stage one, deliberately not skipped.

·

Tracking through occlusion

Partial today, via the three-state tracker above. Substitutes for object permanence in the one-way, no-revisit case.

01

Persistent memory

"I recognize this scene, and it has changed." Nothing currently carries across frames.

02

Plasticity

Learning after deployment — a new object on site, a seasonal change — without retraining from zero.

03

3-D world model

Object permanence — a person behind a tree does not cease to exist and reappear as someone new.

The from-scratch detector was kept as stage one on purpose, not cancelled in favor of these layers — all three consume its output. Building the interesting work on top of nothing would be the worse mistake.

07 Limitations, stated plainly

What is not true yet.

  • No physical Panoptes hardware exists. The helmet, the companion platform, the drone, and the EMG bracelet are unbuilt concepts.
  • The drone AI pilot and the EMG gesture classifier are separate models with no data, no training run, and no scoped timeline.
  • UWB radar has been priced, not simulated, prototyped, or purchased. Affordable consumer radar can not achieve genuine through-wall detection at useful range — treated as a real constraint, not a detail for a future vendor.
  • The camera and thermal fusion test used a synthetic warm-body signal on real background imagery. No thermal hardware is currently owned.

Open questions specific to the detector — the o2o/o2m head gap, the COCO-versus-OpenImages person-AP split, Vigil-Motion's appearance bias — are tracked in the technical report.
